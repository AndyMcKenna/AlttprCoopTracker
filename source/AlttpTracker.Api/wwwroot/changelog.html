ChangelogALTTP Co-op Tracker

Beta 4 — unreleased

  • Keydrop. Tick it in the top bar and the 33 keys under pots and on enemies join their dungeons as checks, the key boxes grow to match, and Hyrule Castle gets its big key. It is a room setting, so everyone in the room sees the same board.
  • The shovel and hammer glyphs on the check tiles — Dig Spot, Digging Game, Hammer Pegs — lose their teal box too.
  • The two Paradox Cave rooms trade names. The randomizer calls the five-chest room Paradox Lower and the two-chest room Paradox Upper, because that is where they are on the mountain — but almost everyone thinks of the five chests as the upper ones, so those names sent people to the wrong room. The tracker now goes with the way people actually talk: Paradox Upper is the five chests, Paradox Lower the two. Anything you had recorded in either room is carried across.

Beta 3 — 13 September 2026

  • The Hammer and Shovel sprites lose the teal box they were cut out with, and sit on the tile like everything else.
  • Right-click anywhere to put down an armed item or check — the same as Esc, without taking a hand off the mouse.
  • Drag the seam between the items and the checks to give either side more room. Double-click it to go back to the default split. The width is remembered in your browser.
  • Heart Container and 300 Rupees join the Equipment group, counting up to the 11 and 4 in the game the way Sword and Bottle do.
  • Death Mountain is its own region, between Light World and Dark World and open like them: the Old Man, Spectacle Rock, the Ether Tablet, Spiral and Mimic Caves, the Paradox chests and the Floating Island. Light World goes from 68 checks to 54. Anything you had recorded up there is carried across.
  • Click All a second time to open every region at once, and again to fold the dungeons back up. All also shows as selected now when no filter is on — it used to be dark on dark.

Beta 2 — 12 September 2026

  • The whole item tile is the button now. Click anywhere on an item to arm it — the little pencil in the corner is gone. The × next to a recorded location still clears just that location.
  • Click in either order. Item then check, as before, or check then item — both record the same thing. The bar at the bottom says which half is still to be clicked, and Esc cancels either.
  • The "Filter checks…" box is gone. Nobody stops to type mid-run; the region chips and the hide switch do the job with one hand still on the controller.
  • Mark a check as dead. The small ∅ at the edge of a check tile says "looked, nothing here": the tile dims for everyone in the room and the hide switch — now "Hide recorded/dead" — takes it off the table. Click a dead check to bring it back if that was a mistake.
  • Items and Keys are two tabs on the left instead of one long column, so the keys no longer mean scrolling. An armed item or check stays armed across the switch.

Beta 1 — 11 September 2026

  • The first public build. A shared board for co-op runs: the 30 main items, the dungeon keys, and all 216 checks, live for everyone in the room.
  • Rooms are kept, so closing the tab and coming back later picks the run up where it was. A room code is three words — generate one on the home page and share the link.
  • Progressive items (Sword, Shield, Mail, Gloves, Bow, Bottle) hold several locations and show a found/total count. A check only ever holds one item, so nobody's note is overwritten.
  • Real item sprites on the tiles, with region filters, search, and a switch to hide checks already recorded.
  • Open source. Report anything wrong at github.com/AndyMcKenna/AlttprCoopTracker.
